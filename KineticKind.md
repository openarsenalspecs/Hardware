# KineticKind  
**The Future of Mobility Is Inclusive.**

KineticKind is an open-source SUV platform designed to solve a critical but overlooked mobility challenge: helping drivers and passengers—especially older adults or people with limited mobility—safely and comfortably enter and exit a vehicle using intelligent seat-assist engineering.

Instead of requiring users to climb out of a car or drop down into it, KineticKind rethinks the entire interaction between human body and vehicle through adaptive motion systems, ergonomic design, and open-source accessibility innovation.

---

## Mission

KineticKind is an open-source vehicle platform created to restore ease, dignity, and independence to everyday mobility through human-centered engineering and community-driven innovation.

We believe mobility should not require struggle. It should adapt to the human body—not force the human body to adapt to the machine.

## Core Mobility & Accessibility Features

- Intelligent lift-assist seating system  
- Rotating seat base for easier entry and exit  
- Optimized ride and seating height for natural standing motion  
- Adaptive ride-height suspension with Easy Entry Mode  
- Wide-access door geometry for reduced twisting and bending  
- Ergonomic grip-path support handles positioned around natural movement points  
- Powered retractable step system with anti-slip surfaces  
- Illuminated step and entry lighting for nighttime safety  
- Transfer Assist Mode for wheelchair and caregiver transfers  
- Powered low-effort door assist with obstacle detection  
- Adjustable standing-height presets for different users  
- Manual emergency override for all accessibility systems  
- Low-strain cabin layout designed around human movement
