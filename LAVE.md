# LAVE
## Low-Altitude Vehicle Experiment

LAVE is an open-source, human-scale hover research platform focused on distributed electric thrust, low-altitude stabilization, and transparent engineering.

This is not a consumer product or commercial eVTOL platform.

LAVE exists to explore the unstable and under-studied space between rolling vehicles and true flight — specifically the first meter above the ground where hover becomes practical, measurable, dangerous, inefficient, and scientifically valuable.

Every subsystem is designed for observability, reproducibility, experimentation, and public research.

No black boxes.  
No hidden optimization.  
No marketing promises.  

Just open engineering and measurable results.

LAVE is designed as a standing, electrically powered hover platform using distributed ducted propulsion, autonomous stabilization systems, and modular open hardware.

- Platform type: standing hover system
